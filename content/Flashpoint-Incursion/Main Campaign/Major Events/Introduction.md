**Roughly 150 years after the [[Solar Civil War|Civil War]],  [[Flashpoint-Incursion/Main Campaign/Factions/Haven/Haven|Haven]] sponsored an exploration to a distant star system.**

The [[HEC Anchor of the Breach|Anchor of the Breach]] was a home to many on this journey to the Drift. It was one of the few vessels in the caravan to hold a [[Flashpoint]] relay, moving it to the deeper expanse, within a loose cluster of possibly habitual planets. The main purpose of this vessel wasn't much of actually using this relay, but as storage in case the others got damaged.

Among those on board, [[Dorian Haster]] was a unique case, having military training, but preferring the profession of engineering. He wasn't the best on the ship, but he knew his way around machines.
