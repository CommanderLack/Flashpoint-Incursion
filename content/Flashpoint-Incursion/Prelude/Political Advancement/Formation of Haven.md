[[Flashpoint-Incursion/Main Campaign/Factions/Haven/Haven|Haven]] is the united force formed after just under a century of [[Solar Civil War|civil war]], where every side was beaten, battered, and unforgivable. Rather than pointing the finger, the new leaders were determined to right their wrongs and focus on support rather than governance, allowing sub-factions to prosper under their protective wing, only asking for the bare necessities it cannot produce for itself.
