**The Terran United Government picked up the pieces of the old United Nations, uniting under one banner in an attempt to cease all future wars.**

Tensions rose as the US and Russia did not stop trying to push each other, eventually sparking a great and terrible war. While both sides restrained themselves from nuclear warfare, much to the relief to civilians everywhere, the fighting continued for such a time, not a few nations crumpled under the pressure. By the time the fighting was over, there was barely a world to fight for. Eventually, the governments fell apart, with civilians and soldiers having to pick up the pieces, using the existing infrastructure to begin rebuilding. This effort required every side to pool what little resources aren't molten slag or useless salvage waiting to be smelted down, slowly forming the Terran United Government.

Once the scattered remains of the world were cleaned up, it was clear resources were thin. Using the limited materials they could scrounge up, a fleet of vessels were made, some cargo, some mining, meant to gather resources from nearby celestial bodies, Mars, the moon, and beyond. Eventually, fuel became easier to produce, more efficient, to where [[Sub-Light Travel]] was actually viable for further distances, allowing more resource gathering, as well as rudimentary mining colonies being formed, slowly becoming more self-sustaining.

After many years, decades... centuries... things slowly changed. The democratic system slowly turned beurocratic and corrupt. This detail was not lost on the outer colonies. Eventually, a [[Solar Civil War|Civil War]] had to break out, repeating the cycle of repair, stability, and eventual war over greed and those suffering from it.
