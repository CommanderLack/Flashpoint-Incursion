During the [[Solar Civil War]], energy shielding was a deciding factor of victory. Before, small, unsuspecting ships could be wiped with just a singular precise shot to reactors or other explosive internals. Now a shield is an effective barrier for damage, however it is not invincible. A shield requires large amounts of energy, necessitating some power output from reactors to be dedicated to shields just for them to be idle.

A shield is not an invincible bubble that can be online forever. Shields are made of hard-light, programmed to vibrate in order to dissipate the kinetic energy of any round. To effectively dissipate energy, it needs a way out, and the purest form of energy is heat. The vibrations can't just go nowhere, there will be some overload, meaning the shield projectors will take up some energy. Heatsinks would be required if captains don't want to permanently lose that projector. The basic thought could be to shoot shields with smaller rounds, overloading the heatsinks by sheer volume, there is another. If a large enough projectile is launched at fast enough speeds, it could pierce through the shields and keep enough mass and momentum to deal actual damage to the hull.
