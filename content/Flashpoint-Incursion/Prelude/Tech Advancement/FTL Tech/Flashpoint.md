The pinnacle of inter-galactic space travel. Unlike the [[Alcubierre drives]], it can {ALMOST} instantly transport anything across any distance using quantum entanglement.

The only downside is it's restricted to relays, sacrificing the [[Alcubierre Drives|Alcubierre drive]]'s freedom for commercialized exploration. If exploring further with node modules, however, the intense gravitational strain of the Alcubierre drive causes it to grow unstable, detonating if done too often, meaning it's recommended only to jump in emergencies, and stay in [[Sub-Light Travel|sub-light]].
