The simplest way we found to travel at quicker speeds. It bends space using heavy amounts of energy to alter gravity so that it's essentially a shorter distance in front of the craft and a further distance behind. It's not entirely pleasant, nor efficient, but it did it's job, improving distance travel, roughly a star system or two per jump. With that distance covered, they collected the materials and funds that enabled them to research the current pinnacle of human exploration technology: quantum tunnelling, coined [[Flashpoint]].
