Before anything, humanity had to create fire. In this instance, commercialized space travel is said fire. While not being the easiest thing, any mechanic could reasonably design their own theoretically functionable design. With the refined rocket science, star ships are just as common as automobiles.

The solar system was quickly colonized afterwards, with shipyards and colonies popping up on every planet and moon, with explorers pushing past into the grander Milky Way. However, they could only go so far before being a one-way trip with no hope of coming back.

Eventually, whether due to ambition or overpopulation, we had to progress further, necessitating the development of faster than light travel, beginning with [[Alcubierre Drives]].
